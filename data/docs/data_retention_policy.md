# Data Retention Policy
Owner: Legal and Compliance. Effective: March 1, 2026.

## Retention periods
Customer shipment records are retained for 7 years after the shipment date. Employee personnel files are retained for 6 years after employment ends. Marketing contact lists are retained for 2 years after the contact's last interaction. System and application logs are retained for 13 months.

## Deletion requests
Customer data deletion requests must be completed within 30 days of verification. Deletion requests are logged in the privacy request tracker, and Legal must be notified of any request that conflicts with a legal hold.

## Legal holds
Data under a legal hold must not be deleted, even if its retention period has expired. Only the General Counsel can place or release a legal hold.
