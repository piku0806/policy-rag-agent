# Information Security Policy
Owner: Security. Effective: February 1, 2026.

## Access control
Multi-factor authentication is required for all company systems. Access to production systems requires approval from the system owner and is reviewed every 90 days. Shared accounts are prohibited.

## Use of AI tools
Employees may use only company-approved AI tools (Microsoft Copilot and the internal AI assistant). Customer data, employee personal data and contract terms must never be pasted into unapproved AI tools. Any AI system that takes actions in production systems must keep an audit log and require human approval for changes to customer-facing records.

## Incident reporting
Suspected security incidents must be reported to the Security Operations Center within 1 hour of discovery, by email to soc@northwind.example or through the incident portal.

## Devices
Company laptops must use full-disk encryption. Lost or stolen devices must be reported within 24 hours.
