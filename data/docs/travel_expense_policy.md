# Travel and Expense Policy
Owner: Finance. Effective: January 1, 2026. Applies to all Northwind Logistics employees and contractors.

## Booking travel
All flights and hotels must be booked through the corporate travel portal (Navan). Economy class is required for flights under 6 hours. Premium economy is allowed for flights of 6 hours or longer. Business class requires written approval from a Vice President before booking.

## Meals and per diem
Domestic meal reimbursement is capped at $75 per day. International meal reimbursement is capped at $110 per day. Alcohol is not reimbursable except at approved client entertainment events.

## Submitting expenses
Expense reports must be submitted within 30 days of the trip end date. Receipts are required for any single expense over $25. Reports submitted after 60 days are not reimbursed unless the CFO grants an exception.

## Approvals
Expense reports under $2,500 are approved by the employee's direct manager. Reports of $2,500 or more also require Finance Director approval.
