# Master Services Agreement Summary: GlobalFreight Carriers
Owner: Procurement. Contract term: April 1, 2026 to March 31, 2029.

## Service levels
GlobalFreight guarantees 96% on-time delivery for full-truckload shipments, measured monthly. If on-time delivery falls below 96% in any month, Northwind receives a service credit of 5% of that month's freight invoice. If it falls below 90%, the service credit is 10%.

## Liability
GlobalFreight's liability for lost or damaged cargo is capped at $100,000 per shipment, unless a higher declared value is purchased in advance. Claims must be filed within 9 months of the delivery date.

## Termination
Either party may terminate for convenience with 90 days' written notice. Northwind may terminate immediately for cause if on-time delivery falls below 90% for three consecutive months.

## Pricing
Rates are fixed for the first 12 months. After that, annual increases are capped at 4% or the change in the US Producer Price Index for truck transportation, whichever is lower.
