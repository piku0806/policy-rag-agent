# Paid Time Off and Leave Policy
Owner: People Team. Effective: January 1, 2026.

## Paid time off
Full-time employees accrue 20 days of paid time off per year. Up to 5 unused days may be carried over into the next calendar year; any additional unused days are forfeited on December 31.

## Parental leave
Primary caregivers receive 16 weeks of fully paid parental leave. Secondary caregivers receive 6 weeks of fully paid parental leave. Parental leave must be taken within 12 months of the birth or adoption.

## Holidays
Northwind observes 11 company holidays each year, published by the People Team every December.
